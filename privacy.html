Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

ClipBot ("the app") is a private, single-user automation tool operated by its owner. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the app accesses

  • YouTube account data — the app uses YouTube API Services (through an OAuth 2.0 token owned by the channel owner) to upload videos and set titles, descriptions and scheduled publish times on the owner's own channel.
  • Video links and files — links provided by the owner to create clips. Videos are downloaded temporarily for processing.
  • Command messages — text messages the owner sends to the app's chat interface, stored locally to keep conversation context.

2. Data the app does not collect

  • No third-party user accounts or personal data.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

3. Storage

All operational data (jobs, clips, tokens, chat history) is stored on infrastructure controlled by the app owner. OAuth tokens are stored in the owner's private configuration and are never exposed publicly.

4. Google / YouTube

The app's use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account permissions.

5. Third-party AI services

Transcripts or command text may be sent to a third-party language model API (Groq) to choose clip moments and generate titles. No personal account data is included in those requests.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions, contact the owner at sanirudra78@gmail.com.
